## Strategy

Use planning/development trackers to find projects early; use issued building permits to confirm that construction is moving. PPRBD is especially valuable because it serves Colorado Springs plus surrounding El Paso County communities and exposes reports and project codes for new SFR, townhomes, duplexes, multifamily and commercial construction.
